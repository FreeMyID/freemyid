Header and footer

The real header and footer on this page are the live components. Recommended header markup:

<header class="site-header"><div class="wrap">
  <a class="brand">…</a>
  <nav class="site-nav" aria-label="Main"><ul>…</ul></nav>
  <details class="menu"><summary>…Menu</summary>
    <nav class="site-nav" aria-label="Main"><ul>… + Download</ul></nav></details>
  <a class="btn btn--primary btn--small site-header__cta">Download</a>
</div></header>

Below 62em the wide nav and .site-header__cta hide and the menu shows, so put Download in the menu list too. Footer: .footer-cols holds four nav columns (h2 + ul), then .footer-legal.
