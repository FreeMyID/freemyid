# Printable checklists

This page is for you, or the person helping you, to print and keep beside the computer.

Each list is one sheet. Tick a box when the step is done.

## Your first week with Free My ID

Date: _______________

1. [ ] Install Free My ID. The steps are in [How to install](INSTALL.md).
2. [ ] Answer the six questions: "Welcome. Who is this for?", "What is the person's name?", "Where do they live?", "A few details to recognize them", "A separate email address for replies", and "Run automatically?". <!-- src/freemyid/wizard/draft.py:67 -->
3. [ ] Read the banner "Practice mode:". It says "Free My ID is looking for your listings, but no removal requests go out until you say so." <!-- src/freemyid/wizard/dashboard.py:237 -->
4. [ ] Look at "Today". <!-- src/freemyid/wizard/ui.py:43 -->
5. [ ] Answer any "Is this you?" card. Press "Yes, this is me" or "No, this is not me". <!-- src/freemyid/wizard/dashboard.py:122 -->
6. [ ] Decide on real requests. Press "Start sending real requests", then "Yes, start sending real requests". <!-- src/freemyid/wizard/dashboard.py:515 -->
7. [ ] Turn on "Run automatically". The button is "Turn on". <!-- src/freemyid/wizard/dashboard.py:502 -->

## Once a month

Date: _______________

1. [ ] Open "Today". <!-- src/freemyid/wizard/ui.py:43 -->
2. [ ] Approve or look at waiting emails. Press "Look at them", then "Approve and send". <!-- src/freemyid/wizard/dashboard.py:255 -->
3. [ ] Check "Protect yourself". <!-- src/freemyid/wizard/ui.py:43 -->
4. [ ] Open What we did for you, then press "Print this page". <!-- src/freemyid/wizard/views.py:301 -->
5. [ ] When a new version is out, install it again. See [How to install](INSTALL.md). "The first time can take up to a minute. Please wait; there is no need to click it again." <!-- packaging/windows/splash_win.py, line 20 and line 23 -->
