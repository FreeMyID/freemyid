# Marketing copy — Free My ID

Draft bank, 2026-11-06. Follows docs/POSITIONING.md (accurate, calm, patriotic not partisan, never mock). Before anything goes public, check every factual line against a cited source.

## Why not "cyber terrorists"

The user's idea was "Free your information from cyber terrorists". The energy is right, but the claim is wrong. The sites we target are legal businesses, so calling them terrorists is false. It invites a defamation letter from a company with lawyers, and it reads like the scam ads this audience already distrusts. What's documented is that **scammers, stalkers and strangers** buy from these sites. That's just as alarming, and it's true. Keep the punch and lose the risk.

## Never use

- "Erase yourself from the internet", "100% removed", "guaranteed"
- "Cyber terrorists", "criminals" or "hackers" to mean the data brokers themselves
- Countdown timers, "act now", red alarm graphics
- Any party, candidate, network or politician
- Claims that we stop phone listening, wiretaps or government surveillance
