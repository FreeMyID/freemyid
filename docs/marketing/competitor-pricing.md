## 2. Recommended prices

| Edition | Recommended | ROADMAP draft | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 forever | $0 | unchanged |
| Plus | **$39/yr** or **$4.99/mo** | ~$49/yr, ~$5/mo | monthly users pay $59.88/yr, the honest nudge to annual |
| Family (up to 6) | **$69/yr** | ~$89/yr | one licence, six profiles |
| Pro (self-host) | **$39/yr**, same feed; a Plus licence also works for Pro | ~$49/yr | one feed, one price |
| Business / Partner | no public price; quote per seat | per seat | see below |

**Plus, $39/yr.** The ROADMAP asks for "clearly cheaper than the big services." At $39 we are under half of Incogni Standard ($95.88, 420+ brokers) and about a third of DeleteMe (about $129, secondary source), and level with Optery Core ($39, 380+ brokers, automated). EasyOptOuts at $19.99 is cheaper and we should not pretend otherwise; we compete on the 400+ daily-updated feed, a real free tier, and nothing stored on our servers. $39 also rounds well for the target reader ("less than $4 a month"), where $49 sits next to Incogni's lower monthly rate. We have no per-customer server cost for a removal run, so margin holds. The $4.99 monthly price is higher per year on purpose, so annual is a real saving and not a trick.

**Family, $69/yr for up to 6.** The comparison families pay $191.88 (Incogni, 5 people), about $329 (DeleteMe, 4 people, secondary) and about $384 (Aura, 5 adults). $69 is about 12 dollars per person and under two Plus licences, which makes the family plan the obvious choice for the adult child setting up parents, our main buyer. $89 would still be cheapest of the big services but weakens the "set up everyone for the price of two" story. Review at 0.5 once support cost per household is known.

**Pro, $39/yr.** Pro users run the same code headless on a home server and need the same signed feed as Plus. Keeping the price identical removes a confusing question ("why does Linux cost more?") and avoids paying for what is mostly self-support: Pro gets community and issues, not email. A Plus licence covering Pro means a person with a PC and a homelab pays once. Dropping from $49 recognises that Pro buyers are the group most able to self-build the feed from the AGPL code and who must be kept willing to pay.

**Business.** Do not publish a price. Guide for quotes only (guess, not market data): per-seat, annual, low single-digit to low double-digit dollars at volume, with a minimum (Kanary Team requires 5 seats). Pricing is orchestrator and sales territory; nothing public until the paid tier is announced.

**Terms recommended with these prices:** 30-day no-questions refund (matches Incogni, Optery, Privacy Bee); renewal reminder email 14 days before charge; cancel in the app in two clicks; price locked for the life of an unbroken subscription (flag to orchestrator: needs a business decision); no auto-upgrade of plan.
