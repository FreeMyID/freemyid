## FAQ

**Why is it free?**
Because looking after your own privacy should not need a subscription. A small share of people want the full list kept up to date for them and are happy to pay for that. Their payments keep the free version going. That is the whole plan.

**What do you do with my data?**
Your details are kept on your own computer. There is no account and no cloud copy. Free My ID sends your details only to the sites you ask it to remove you from, because they need them to find your listing. We do not receive them, we do not sell them and we do not run ads. If you pay, the payment company handles your card, and we see that you have a licence and an email for receipts. (CONFIRM what the processor shares with us.)

**Can I cancel?**
Yes, anytime, inside the app, in two clicks. You keep Plus until the end of what you paid for. If you ask within 30 days of paying, we refund you.

**Will it remove me from everything?**
No. It asks sites to take your listing down, and most do, but not all, and some put people back later. That is why Free My ID checks again. It reports what was asked, what was confirmed and what is still waiting. It does not stop phone calls to a wiretap or hacking, and it is not identity-theft protection.

**Do I need Plus?**
Probably not to start. Try the free version first. If you find you want more sites and faster re-checks, Plus is there.

**What if I stop paying?**
You go back to Free. Your records stay on your computer.

**I run it on a Linux server. Is that Plus?**
That is the Pro edition: $39 a year for the same full list, community support. A Plus licence also works for Pro.
