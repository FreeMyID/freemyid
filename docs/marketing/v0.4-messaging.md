# v0.4 "For the family" messaging brief (DRAFT)

Author: Jordan Ellis, 2026-11-08. Internal. Nothing here is posted or published. Source of scope: docs/ROADMAP.md (v0.4 line 207; rows cited below) and docs/BACKLOG.md lane IN (BL-IN-05, 20, 21, 23, 24, 25). Copy rules: docs/POSITIONING.md and the "Never use" list in docs/MARKETING_COPY.md.

## The one idea
0.3 is "see it, fix it" (you). 0.4 is "for the family": the person who sets this up for a parent, and the parent who wants to see, on one page, what was done. Audience is the helper (adult child, grandchild) and the older relative. Keep it quiet and practical, no fear.

## Rule for every line below
"Coming" is allowed on the What's coming page and in the roadmap only. "Available" requires a published release that contains the item (ROADMAP section 6). No dates and no download button for unreleased items. Every release-day line gets a commit tag, as in release-notes-v0.3.0.md.

Check each clause against the shipped build.

**Objections to answer on the page:** Is it safe to set it up for a parent? (their details stay on their computer; helper bundle is encrypted if that is what ships, verify). Does it spy on them?
