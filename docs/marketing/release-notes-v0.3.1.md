# Free My ID 0.3.1

A small update to 0.3.0. If you already have Free My ID, the new installer updates it and keeps your details.

**What's fixed**

- **The first start no longer looks stuck.** The first time Free My ID opens, it can take up to a minute to get ready. Before, the "starting" window closed too early, and the screen stayed empty or white for a while. Now the "starting" window stays until Free My ID is ready. If it takes longer than two minutes, the window says it is still starting and that there is no need to click it again.
- **Clearer words in practice mode.** Two messages said Free My ID was "not sending anything". That was not quite right: in practice mode it still looks you up on people-search sites. They now say it is looking for your listings, but no removal requests go out until you say so.

**What has not changed**

- No Free My ID account, no cloud, no tracking. Your details are stored on your computer.
- The installer is not yet code-signed, so Windows may show a blue "unknown publisher" box. The download page explains how to check the file.

Questions or something confusing? Use "Tell us what confuses you" on freemyid.org. You do not need an account.
