# Remove yourself from SmartBackgroundChecks: free steps (DRAFT)

Slug: `smartbackgroundchecks` | Status: draft for web team, not live | Last checked: 2026-11-08 (broker page fetched on this date)

**Intro (plain words).** SmartBackgroundChecks is a people-search site: it shows names, addresses and phone numbers gathered from public records. You can ask it to take your listing down. No payment is part of these steps.

## Opt-out link
https://www.smartbackgroundchecks.com/optout (broker's own page, fetched 2026-11-08) [1]

## What it asks for
- Your name and an email address, and a "not a robot" check. [1]
- Then a short form: first, last and optional middle name, and an email. Your details "should match the information on the record you want removed". [1]
- No ID and no phone number are mentioned on the page. [1]
- Free My ID never asks for your SSN, bank or card details, or a payment (see /safety/). The removal shouldn't need them either: if a site asks for any of these, stop and close it.

## Steps
1. Open the link above.
2. Type your name and an email address. A separate email just for this is best.
3. Tick the box that lets SmartBackgroundChecks email you about the request, do the "not a robot" check, and press **Submit**. [2]
4. Open the email they send and click the link in it. It stops working after 24 hours; if so, start again. [1]
5. Fill in the short form so it matches your listing, then press its **Submit** button. [2] (UNVERIFIED: secondary source)
6. You see a confirmation page and get a confirmation email. Keep it. [1]

## How long
The page says to "allow 3 days for your record to be fully removed". [1]

## Good to know
Sites like this can list people again later. UNVERIFIED for this site specifically; broker-researcher to confirm before we say it here. (Where this site is in your copy of Free My ID's site list, it rechecks for you. SmartBackgroundChecks is not in the public sample list yet, so check your own list.)
