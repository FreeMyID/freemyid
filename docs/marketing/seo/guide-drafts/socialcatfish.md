# Remove yourself from Social Catfish: free steps (DRAFT)

Slug: `socialcatfish` | Status: draft | Last checked: 2026-11-08

**Intro.** Social Catfish is a search site that looks people up by name, email, phone, username or photo. It has a removal form. Its page also advertises a paid service; you do not need it for this request.

## Opt-out link
https://www.socialcatfish.com/opt-out/ (broker's own page, fetched 2026-11-08) [1]

## What it asks for
- Required: first name, last name and email. [1]
- Optional: links to your Social Catfish profiles, age range, other emails, phone numbers to remove, photos to remove (up to 8). [1] FLAG: phone numbers and photos are optional. Only add a phone number you want removed.
- No ID upload up front, but if a request "may be fraudulent" the site "may seek information to verify your identity". [1] FLAG: possible identity check later; UNVERIFIED what it asks.
- Never asks for: SSN, bank or card details, a payment. The page promotes a paid "Privacy Lock" service; that is an ad, skip it. [1]

## Steps
1. Open the link above.
2. Pick the state where you live now.
3. Fill in first name, last name and email.
4. Tick the "authorized agent" box only if you are doing this for an organization (most people: leave it blank).
5. Press "Submit Now".

## How long
It says the opt-out takes effect "within fifteen (15) days". [1]

## Good to know
The page says removal "only fixes one site". [1]
