## Opt-out link
https://www.peoplesmart.com/, then the footer link "Do Not Sell / Share My Personal Information", which per [1] redirects to the BeenVerified opt-out page. UNVERIFIED.

## What it asks for (per [1])
- First and last name, state, an email address, and a CAPTCHA. [1]
- No ID or phone number mentioned. [1]
- Free My ID never asks for your SSN, bank or card details, or a payment (see /safety/). The removal shouldn't need them either: if a site asks for any of these, stop and close it.

## Steps (per [1], UNVERIFIED)
1. Open the site, click the footer link above.
2. Enter your first and last name and state, press Search.
3. Press "Proceed to Opt Out" next to your listing.
4. Enter your email, do the CAPTCHA, press "Send Verification Email".
5. Open the email (check spam) and click "Verify Opt-Out".

## How long
Not stated by the guide [1]. Other vendors say 72 hours to 7 business days [2]; UNVERIFIED.
