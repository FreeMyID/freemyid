**Intro.** CyberBackgroundChecks is a people-search site showing names, addresses and phone numbers. It has a removal form.

Sourcing note: cyberbackgroundchecks.com returned "403 Forbidden" to our fetch tool, so facts below are from a third-party guide and are UNVERIFIED until checked in a real browser.

## Opt-out link
https://www.cyberbackgroundchecks.com/removal [1]. UNVERIFIED: another vendor lists /ccparemoval [2].

## What it asks for
- First, middle and last name and an email, plus a "not a robot" check. [1]
- On the second form: "personal information" (fields not named by the guide). Phone number is optional ("it is not mandatory"). [1] FLAG: may ask for a phone number and date of birth; the phone is optional. Another summary says the form also has date of birth and address [2], UNVERIFIED.
- No ID mentioned. [1]
- Never asks for: SSN, bank or card details, a payment.

## Steps (per [1], UNVERIFIED)
1. Open the link above.
2. Type your name and email, do the check, submit.
3. Open the verification email (check spam) and click its link. It is "only valid for 24 hours". [1]
4. Fill in the Record Suppression Form and submit.

## How long
Guide says "3 days or less" [1]; Incogni says 48 hours [2]. UNVERIFIED; do not publish until the broker's page is seen.
