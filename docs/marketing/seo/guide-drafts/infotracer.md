# Remove yourself from InfoTracer: free steps (DRAFT)

Slug: `infotracer` | Status: draft | Last checked: 2026-11-08

**Intro.** InfoTracer is a background-check and people-search site. Its own page gives several ways to ask for deletion but no single walk-through, so this guide is short and flags the gaps.

## Opt-out link
https://infotracer.com/optout/ (broker's own page, fetched 2026-11-08) [1]

## What it asks for
- Ordinary request: a request form (a TrustArc form) linked as "HERE" on the page. It says it "may need to ask you additional questions to confirm the information associated with you". [1] FLAG: it may ask extra questions to confirm who you are; the page does not say what they are. UNVERIFIED.
- No ID, phone number or payment is mentioned. [1]
- Free My ID never asks for your SSN, bank or card details, or a payment (see /safety/). The removal shouldn't need them either: if a site asks for any of these, stop and close it.

## Steps
1. Open the link above.
2. Find "To submit a request ... please contact us HERE" and open that form. [1]
3. Ask for deletion of your information. The form's fields were not shown on the page. UNVERIFIED.
4. Judges, prosecutors, law enforcement and their immediate family can instead email privacy@infotracer.com with the subject "Attention Covered Person Removal Request" and include complete name, residence, email and status. [1]

## How long
"We will respond to your request within 45 days, or notify you if additional time is required." That is a reply time, not a removal time. [1]
