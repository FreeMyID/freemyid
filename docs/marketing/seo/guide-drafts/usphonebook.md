# Remove yourself from USPhoneBook: free steps (DRAFT)

Slug: `usphonebook` | Status: draft, NOT READY (broker page could not be opened by us) | Last checked: 2026-11-08

**Intro.** USPhoneBook is a phone and people lookup site. You can ask it to remove a listing.

Sourcing note: our fetch tool was blocked from usphonebook.com, so every fact below comes from a third-party guide and is UNVERIFIED until broker-researcher opens the broker's page in a real browser.

## Opt-out link
https://www.usphonebook.com/opt-out, reached from the "Do Not Sell My Personal Information" link in the site footer [1]. UNVERIFIED: another guide says the page is usphonebook.com/removal [2]; check which one loads.

## What it asks for
- An email address for the confirmation, a "not a robot" check, and your phone number to find your listing. [1] FLAG: it asks for a phone number to look you up. That is the number already on your listing; you are not adding a new one.
- No government ID mentioned. [1]
- Never asks for: SSN, bank or card details, a payment.

## Steps (per [1], UNVERIFIED)
1. Open usphonebook.com, go to the bottom, click "Do Not Sell My Personal Information".
2. Enter an email address (a separate one for removals is best) and do the check.
3. Type your phone number and search. Open the matching profile.
4. Make sure it is you, then click "Remove Record".
5. Open the confirmation email (look in spam too) and click its link. One guide says the link works for 24 hours. [2]
6. Wait for the confirmation page.

## How long
UNVERIFIED. The guide says 72 hours in one place and 48 in another [1]; others say 24 to 72 hours [2]. The broker's own statement was not seen. Do not publish a number until verified.
