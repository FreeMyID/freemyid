# Remove yourself from CheckPeople: free steps (DRAFT)

Slug: `checkpeople` | Status: draft for web team, not live | Last checked: 2026-11-08

**Intro.** CheckPeople is a people-search site. It has a "Suppression Center" for asking it to hide your public listing.

## Opt-out link
https://www.checkpeople.com/opt-out (broker's own page, fetched 2026-11-08) [1]

## What it asks for
- Only an email address at the first step, plus a tick box agreeing to its Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. [1]
- The box also agrees to "transactional emails" about the tool. [1]
- No ID and no phone number are mentioned on that page. [1]
- Never asks for: SSN, bank or card details, a payment.

## Steps
1. Open the link above.
2. Type your email address. A separate email just for removals is best.
3. Tick the box to agree to the terms, then press Continue.
4. Open the verification email and click its link to go on. [1]
5. UNVERIFIED: the steps after the email link (finding your record and confirming). We did not see those pages. broker-researcher to walk through them before publishing.
