# Remove yourself from Social Catfish: free steps (DRAFT)

Slug: `social-catfish` | Status: draft | Last checked: 2026-11-08

**Intro.** Social Catfish is a search site that looks people up by name, email, phone, username or photo. It has a removal form. Its page also advertises a paid service; you do not need it for this request.

## Opt-out link
https://www.socialcatfish.com/opt-out/ (broker's own page, fetched 2026-11-08) [1]

## What it asks for
- Required: first name, last name and email. [1]
- The form lists links to your Social Catfish profiles as optional, but Social Catfish's terms say you must submit all URLs with your information. UNVERIFIED which is right; broker-researcher to test the live form. To be safe, search the site for your name, copy the address of each profile that is you, and paste them in if the form has a box for them. Other optional items: age range, other emails, phone numbers to remove, photos to remove (up to 8). [1] FLAG: phone numbers and photos are optional. Only add a phone number you want removed.
- No ID upload up front, but if a request "may be fraudulent" the site "may seek information to verify your identity". [1] FLAG: possible identity check later; UNVERIFIED what it asks.
- Free My ID never asks for your SSN, bank or card details, or a payment (see /safety/). The removal shouldn't need them either: if a site asks for any of these, stop and close it. The page promotes a paid "Privacy Lock" service; that is an ad, skip it. [1]

## Steps
1. Open the link above.
2. Pick the state where you live now.
3. Fill in first name, last name and email. If the form has a box for profile links, paste the address of each of your Social Catfish profiles there (UNVERIFIED that the form has one; see above).
4. Tick the "authorized agent" box only if you are doing this for an organization (most people: leave it blank).
5. Press "Submit Now".
6. Verify your email address: Social Catfish's terms say "You will need to verify your email address and submit all URLs with your information" for an opt-out. Look for their email (check spam too) and follow it. [2] How the current form does the check: UNVERIFIED.

## How long
It says the opt-out takes effect "within fifteen (15) days". [1]

## Good to know
The page says removal "only fixes one site". [1]
