## Ready-to-post messages (use only after the status allows)

**Scam / safety servers (D2, D3, D5):**
> I work on Free My ID, a free open-source Windows app. If you or a relative are getting calls and mail from people who found your address and phone number, they probably came from people-search sites. The app asks those sites to take your listing down, runs on your own computer (no account, nothing sent to us) and re-checks, because sites re-list people. It never asks for an SSN, card or bank details. It does not stop phone scams or hacking. Early release: https://freemyid.org . Feedback welcome.

**Privacy / security servers (D4):**
> I work on Free My ID, an AGPL-3.0 local-first app that sends data-broker opt-outs by email, a guided step or an automatic browser form, then re-checks on a schedule. No account, no telemetry service. Windows only so far; early release. Code: https://github.com/FreeMyID/freemyid . Limits: people-search and broker sites only, and some sites will not comply. Architecture and threat-model criticism welcome.

**Senior and beginner tech help (D1, D5, D6):**
> I work on Free My ID, a free Windows app built so your grandparents can use it. It asks the websites that list your name, address and phone number to take you off, and keeps checking. No account. Step-by-step guides also work without the app: https://freemyid.org/guides/ . Happy to answer questions.

**Homesteading, prepping, self-reliance (D7 to D12):**
> I work on Free My ID, a free, open-source app that runs on your own computer, with no cloud and no account. It asks the sites that sell your name, address and phone number to take your listing down, then re-checks. It will not stop wiretaps or hacking; it removes listings from people-search sites. Early release: https://freemyid.org . Feedback welcome.
