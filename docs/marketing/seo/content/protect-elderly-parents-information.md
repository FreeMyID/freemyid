# Help a parent get their details off people-search sites

If your mom, dad or grandparent's name, address and phone number show up on people-search sites, you can help them ask for it to be taken down. It is free, it takes an afternoon, and you do not need to be technical. [S1]

## Get their permission first

Do this with them, not for them. Each site asks the person on the listing to confirm, and some send a confirmation email to an address you will need to open together. Use a new, free email address set up for removals so confirmation links and any follow-up mail stay out of their main inbox. [S1]

## What you need

- Their name and city, spelled the way the listing shows it. [S1]
- The removal email address you set up.
- A list of sites to work through. Start with the ones that show up first when you search their name. Our site-by-site guides are at https://freemyid.org/guides/. [S1]

## Never share these

No genuine opt-out page needs a Social Security number, a bank account or a card number. If a page asks for any of them, stop. [S1]

## Check back

Some sites list people again after a while, so search their name again every few months and repeat the steps for anything that came back. [S2]

## If they would rather not do it by hand

Free My ID is a free, open-source Windows app that sends the removal requests for you and re-checks on a schedule. Their details stay on their computer and go only to the sites they ask to remove them. There is no account. It works on people-search and data-broker sites only. https://freemyid.org/families/ [S1]
