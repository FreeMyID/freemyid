## 1. What a freeze does, in two sentences
Stops new credit accounts being opened using your file until you lift it. Source: FTC, "while a freeze is in place nobody can open a new credit account" (https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/credit-freezes-and-fraud-alerts). Does NOT remove you from people-search sites: link to /guides/ hub (separate problem). Claim "does not affect existing accounts": needs-verify.

## 2. It costs nothing
FTC: "There's no cost to place or lift a credit freeze" and it lasts until you lift it (same URL).

## 3. Before you start (checklist box)
- Have ready: name, address, date of birth, SSN (the bureaus ask; Free My ID never does). needs-verify exact fields per bureau.
- A phone, email and a paper and pen for the PIN or password each bureau gives you.
- Plan: one bureau at a time, same afternoon.

## 4. Step by step, per bureau
Equifax, Experian, TransUnion: contact links at https://www.identitytheft.gov/#/CreditBureauContacts (named by FTC page). Per-bureau steps and phone numbers: needs-verify by broker-researcher (fetch each bureau's official page, record date).

## 5. The extra bureaus
Innovis, ChexSystems, NCTUE, LexisNexis: what each covers and how to freeze. needs-verify (ROADMAP line 97 lists them; no sources gathered yet).

## 6. Helping a parent
- Do it together, parent present, at their kitchen table; never ask for their SSN by chat.
- Write the PINs on a sheet kept somewhere safe at home.
- Guardianship/protected-consumer freeze for someone who can't act: needs-verify, sourced from the bureaus or CFPB.

## 7. Lifting it when they apply for credit
FTC: identify which bureau the lender will use and lift only there, then put it back (same URL).
