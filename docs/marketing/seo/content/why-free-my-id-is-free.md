Free My ID is free. Not a trial, not "free for 7 days". If you are one person who wants your name, address and phone number taken off people-search sites, you can use it for as long as you like and pay nothing.

You should ask why. Most companies in this space charge a yearly fee, and some of the free ones earn money in ways you would not like. Here is our plain answer.

## What is free, and stays free

- The app for individuals: the setup, the dashboard, and the removal requests by email and with step-by-step help.
- The code. Free My ID is open source (AGPL-3.0), so anyone can read it, check it, and copy it.
- The safety basics: guides for freezing your credit, checking breaches, and opting out by hand. We will not put these behind a payment.

## We do not make money from your details

Your details stay on your computer, encrypted. They go only to the sites you ask to remove you. Our servers hold none of your personal information, so there is nothing for us to sell, and we have no way to. That is a design rule, not a promise we hope to keep: anything we run ourselves is built to hold no personal data.

## If we ever charge, here is what for

Free My ID is a project that has to pay its bills eventually. The owner has decided the line, and it is this: **the free and paid line is freshness and convenience, never safety.**

The code is free. What we may sell later is the constantly updated knowledge of how each site works, and extra convenience on top, such as faster re-checks, more hands-off automation, and tools for a family or an organization. Think of how an antivirus program works: the program is one thing, and the regular updates are another.

If you never pay anything, you still get the core set of the biggest sites, checked every 90 days, with the same promise about your data.

## How we will stay honest

- **No scare tactics.** No fear pop-ups, no fake counters, no "your data is being sold right now" alarms.
- **No traps.** No auto-renewing surprises or bundled extras.
- **Plain words.** We say "requested", "confirmed" or "relisted". We never say your information is "erased" or promise a result we cannot control, because we cannot make a site comply.
- **You decide.** Nothing is sent in your name until you say go. A practice mode is on by default.
- **Open code.** If we ever break these promises, you can see it in the code and you can walk away with a copy.
- **Not a "we do it for you" service.** We chose not to run one for now, because it would mean holding customers' personal details. We would rather not have them.

## What this is not

It is an early release for Windows 10 and 11. It works on people-search and data-broker sites, not on Facebook or Google themselves, and no tool can promise every site will remove you. Our guides say so too.

Questions or complaints are welcome on our GitHub page: https://github.com/FreeMyID/freemyid
