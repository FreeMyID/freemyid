# Competitor website review for the in-house revamp

Status: DRAFT, internal, inspiration only. Author: Jordan Ellis (brand-marketing). Checked 2026-11-08.
Rules followed: no text, images, logos or layouts copied; no competitor name or mark goes into our copy; every observation below is "what the page showed on the date" with its URL. No data was entered anywhere.

**Method and limits.** Pages were read through an automated fetch that returns a text summary, not screenshots. So visual notes are inferred from page structure, and every number below is *as shown on the page that day*. Verify in a real browser before quoting any figure publicly. Prices are in `docs/marketing/competitor-pricing.md` (not repeated here). Several deep pages returned 404 or were not reachable; that is noted per site.

## 2. Top 10 ideas for our pages, ranked by impact versus effort

All ideas use existing components (see `web/src/components/README.md`), stay script-free, and use no stock photos, counters or urgency. Copy in quotes is a draft for the section owner to adapt, not to paste without a check against `docs/POSITIONING.md`.

| # | Idea | Page and section | Why it helps older, non-technical readers | Component | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **"What it does and does not do" as a two-column honest list, with the limits first.** Borrowed principle: the candid FAQs on two sites build credibility. Ours: "It can't remove everything. Sites can list you again. It doesn't touch Facebook." | How it works, "what it does / doesn't do"; also Safety `#limits` | This audience is scam-wary; stating limits is the strongest anti-scam signal. Reduces "so it will erase me?" disappointment. | `CheckList` pair in two `Card`s, or `BentoGrid` (one `tone="soft"`) | Low (copy exists) |
| 2 | **One number, defined, in the hero trust line.** Do not copy big counters; use one fact we can prove from the registry or code, with a footnote saying what it counts. | Home hero trust line and `StatGroup` | Competitors' undefined counters read as marketing; a defined number reads as plain fact. Must be sourced (broker-researcher confirms) and carry the registry date. | `StatGroup` / `Stat` with source comment | Low |
| 3 | **A "price: $0, account: none, sends to us: nothing" row directly under the download button.** Three short facts, no jargon. | Home hero; Download page top | Every competitor asks for sign-up first. Answering "what's the catch?" before the ask is the trust-before-ask rule. | `Badge` row or small `StatGroup` | Low |
| 4 | **The full covered-sites list, plain and alphabetical, with a "last checked" date and a note on what each way (Email / Do it with me / Fill it in) means.** Borrowed principle: one site publishes an alphabetical list with an update date. Ours adds tiers and honesty about sites we cover by guide only. | Guides index; Home `#guide-marquee` links to it | A named list beats "1000+ sites" for people who want to find their own sites. Registry count and date must come from `src/`/registry, not typed by hand. | Existing `Marquee` plus a `FeatureGrid`/`Disclosure` list on Guides | Medium |
| 5 | **Timing and re-listing stated in the process, as part of step three.** "Most sites answer in days to weeks (some state law deadlines are 45 days where a law applies). Sites sometimes list you again, so it checks again." | How it works `#steps` timeline, step 3; FAQ | Two sites state realistic timing and re-adding in the process; it prevents "it didn't work" panic. Ours is plain words with a "verify it yourself" link. | Home (after the "wrong place" band); Safety top | Trust before ask. Plain-language equivalent of badge rows: the badge strips used elsewhere are certifications we do not hold; do not imitate them. | `FeatureGrid cols={4}` with `icon-tile` | Low |

Cuts reading load. | `Disclosure` | Low |

Place it before the SmartScreen steps. | Download `#smartscreen` and `#checksum` lead-in | Direct answer to "is this a virus?". Competitors never need this because they are web sign-ups; for an unsigned exe, being first with the explanation is our best trust move. | `Alert variant="info"` + existing `SmartScreen` and `CopyHash` | Low (copy exists) |

Why rank this way: 1, 3, 5, 8, 9, 10 are mostly copy and existing components (hours). 2, 4 need a registry-sourced number or list (verify first). 6 needs art. 7 needs confirmation of the feature.

Ideas considered and rejected: "as seen on" logo strips and review carousels (we have no press or reviews; fake social proof is banned); sale banners and promo codes; email-gated discounts and pop-ups; floating chat widgets; counters; phone-number scan forms; persona fear blocks ("scammers, identity thieves, data aggregators").

## 3. Copy angles where our honest differences win (drafts, within POSITIONING rules)

We name no competitor. The contrast is against the common pattern, which we have documented above for ten sites. Each line is literally true of the current build; check the "needs" note first.

| Our difference | Angle (draft) | Contrast it answers | Needs before it ships |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | "Free. No trial, no card, no 'upgrade to see your results'." | Free scans that lead to a paid plan; trials and promo banners | Orchestrator OK that nothing on the public site mentions a future paid tier (per standing rule) |
| No account | "No sign-up. We never ask for your email address to start." | Email or phone number demanded before you see anything | Confirm wizard asks for no account; wording about the removal mailbox must stay accurate |
| Local | "Your details stay on your computer. They go only to the sites you ask to remove you." | "Hand us your profile" model | Keep exact wording from POSITIONING (never "never leaves your computer") |
| Open source | "You do not have to take our word for it. The code is public, so anyone can read it." | Unverifiable "trusted by millions" and unexplained badges | Wording must say source is readable, not "audited" or "certified" |
| Practice mode | "Try it first. Nothing is sent until you say so." | No competitor shown with a rehearsal | wizard-packager confirms behaviour |
| Honest numbers | "We say how many sites we cover and the date we last checked. No round numbers." | Counts that differ between title, hero and FAQ on the same site | Registry count and date from `src/`, not hand-typed |
| Honest limits | "It can't fix Facebook, a phone that listens, or hackers. Here's what it does fix." | Overpromising "never give out your info again" language | POSITIONING Lines we do NOT cross |
| Calm | "No countdown. No red alert. No salesperson." | Sale banners, countdown language, fear lists | None; the page must also follow it (it already bans these) |
| Family-friendly | "Set it up once for a parent. The steps are big, plain and in order." | Dashboards aimed at the buyer, not the user | Families page owner confirms the claim |

Do not say: "cheaper than", "better than", "the only", "private" in the sense of anonymity, "erase", "guaranteed", "100%", or anything about identity-theft protection, and no price comparison on the public site until the orchestrator says the paid tier is announced.

- Not reachable (404): Incogni /how-it-works/ and /faq/-style pages, Optery /faq/, Mozilla Monitor /faq, one Aura learn article. Trust or security pages were read only for Cloaked. DeleteMe, Optery, Kanary and others expose trust signals on the home page instead (badges, pledges).

- Permission Slip's page was read through the redirect to its new host; whether Consumer Reports still runs the app was not stated there and is not claimed here.
