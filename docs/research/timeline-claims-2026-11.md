## Part 1. WR-61 and WR-62 answers

### WR-61 (protect-elderly-parents-information.md), row S2
Claim: "Some sites list people again after a while, so search their name again every few months."
Verdict: CLEARED, with the wording kept as is (it says "some sites" and "can", not "will").
Sources:
- Spokeo Compass blog, "How Our Opt-Out System Works" (page, https://www.spokeo.com/compass/how-spokeo-opt-out-system-works/). Spokeo says an opted-out listing is flagged "so that it does not ever reappear", but a new record with a different address or typos may not match the old opt-out, so "you will see a new listing for your name". It recommends "checking back on our site for new listings periodically". The article is from 2011 with 2012-13 comments, so it is old.
- Spokeo, "Listing Removal" (page, https://www.spokeo.com/compass/spokeo-removal/): same reply, "a new listing can still show up (not a reappearance of a previously opted-out listing)" when data cannot be matched; advises checking periodically.
- California Privacy Protection Agency, "How DROP works" (page, https://privacy.ca.gov/drop/how-drop-works/): "After initial processing, data brokers must re-check and delete new matching data at least every 45 days." This is the regulator saying new matching data keeps arriving.
Do not say "every few months" is a rule. It is our advice. Suggested wording if the writer wants a source on the interval: "Some sites can list you again under a new record, so check your name again now and then."

### WR-62 (who-is-selling-my-phone-number.md)
- S2 "Sites remove the whole profile on request": NOT CLEARED as stated. The opt-out forms I can read (Spokeo, PeopleConnect, TruePeopleSearch, BeenVerified per registry notes) are per listing or per record: Spokeo says each listing is a separate request, and BeenVerified allows one record online. Spokeo's help article "Can I remove my phone number on Spokeo?" (https://help.spokeo.com/hc/en-us/articles/44038323312531-Can-I-remove-my-phone-number-on-Spokeo) exists but returned 403, so I cannot say what it states. Suggested wording: "Most removal pages ask you to pick the listing that shows your number and remove that listing. Each listing is a separate request, and a site can have more than one listing for you."
- S3 "Sites can list people again": CLEARED, same sources as WR-61 S2.
- S4 Do Not Call scope: CLEARED with this wording. Source (page): FTC, "National Do Not Call Registry FAQs", https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/national-do-not-call-registry-faqs. The FTC says the registry stops unwanted sales calls from real companies that follow the law. It "won't stop calls from scammers making illegal calls", and the rules still allow political, charity, debt-collection, purely informational and survey calls (as long as they carry no sales pitch). It can take "up to 31 days for sales calls to stop". Registration "will never expire". To report unwanted calls (no money lost) use the form at https://www.donotcall.gov/; if you lost money to a scam use https://reportfraud.ftc.gov. Suggested text: "The FTC's Do Not Call registry stops sales calls from companies that follow the law. It will not stop scammers, and political, charity, survey and debt-collection calls are still allowed. It can take up to 31 days to take effect. Report unwanted calls at donotcall.gov."
  Note: the draft says removing listings "does not touch your carrier". Fine, no source needed (it is a limit, not a claim of effect).
- S1 first paragraph ("Typing a phone number into a people-search site often brings up a name and address") and "Phone numbers reach these sites from public records, marketing lists and other data sources": no primary source gathered. Spokeo's own help page on phone numbers returned 403. Keep the draft's own hedge ("We do not claim to know which source...") and consider "often" -> "can", or cite the ODNI report already used in faq.yaml for the general point that data is commercially bought and sold.

## Part 2. Timeline claims table

Regulatory baselines, read today (page):
- CCPA deletion/know requests: "Businesses must respond to your request within 45 calendar days. They can extend that deadline by another 45 days (90 days total) if they notify you." California Attorney General, https://oag.ca.gov/privacy/ccpa (Section D, FAQ 3).
- DROP: from 2026-08-01 brokers must begin processing; they "must re-check and delete new matching data at least every 45 days"; "It could take up to 90 days to see status updates in DROP." https://privacy.ca.gov/drop/how-drop-works/.
- Other states' 45-day windows are in brokers/state_laws.yaml (statute text, Pass 5).
- Spokeo: privacy policy says opt-out requests are "processed in real time" (snippet, https://www.spokeo.com/privacy-policy); an older FAQ said removed within 1 business day and the oldest opt-out page 24-48 hours (snippet, https://www.spokeo.com/optout and https://www.spokeo.com/faqs-consumer, both 403 to the fetcher). The registry note (browser check 2026-11-07) says 24-48 hours.
- TruePeopleSearch: confirmation page says "Expect your information to be fully removed in 3 days or less" (registry success_text, browser check 2026-11-07; not re-fetched here, site blocked).
- Spokeo's blog pages give no timeframe.
