How to remove yourself from Equifax

Equifax is a data company listed on California's data broker registry. Here's how to ask it to remove you, for free. You don't need Free My ID to do this.

Step by step

  1. Open the privacy portal page.

  2. Fill in your name and address, then pick the choices you want.

  3. Or call 1-866-295-6801.

Open Equifax's removal page

Opens myprivacy.equifax.com, Equifax's own website.

We checked these steps against Equifax's own page on 2026-11-05.

Or let Free My ID do it

Free My ID opens Equifax's removal page in your own browser, with these steps beside it and a Copy button for each box. On sites that allow it, it can fill the boxes in while you watch. You tick any "I'm not a robot" box yourself. Then it checks again later, because sites can list people again.
