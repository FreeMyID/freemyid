How to remove yourself from PeopleConnect Suppression Center

PeopleConnect Suppression Center is a people-search site. Sites like this build listings from public records and purchased data, often with your address, age, phone number and relatives. Here's how to ask it to remove you, for free. You don't need Free My ID to do this.

What you'll need

  • Your name, as it shows on the listing
  • An email address for removals (a separate one just for this is best)
  • A few minutes to open the email PeopleConnect Suppression Center sends and click its link. If you skip this, the request may not go through.

Step by step

  1. Open the Suppression Center page.

  2. Type your name and removal email address, and find your record.

  3. Select your record, then confirm.

  4. Open the email they send and click its link.

  5. This covers Intelius, Instant Checkmate, TruthFinder, US Search and ZabaSearch. It does not cover Classmates.

Allow about 7 days, then check again.

Open PeopleConnect Suppression Center's removal page

Opens suppression.peopleconnect.us, PeopleConnect Suppression Center's own website.

We haven't been able to check these steps against PeopleConnect Suppression Center's live page yet. Sites change their pages often, so if something looks different, follow what the page says.

The PeopleConnect Suppression Center removal is also meant to cover: Intelius, TruthFinder.

You should never have to pay to be removed. If a people-search site asks for money or your Social Security number, stop.

Or let Free My ID do it

Free My ID opens PeopleConnect Suppression Center's removal page in your own browser, with these steps beside it and a Copy button for each box. On sites that allow it, it can fill the boxes in while you watch. You tick any "I'm not a robot" box yourself. Then it checks again later, because sites can list people again.
