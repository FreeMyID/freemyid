How to remove yourself from LiveRamp

LiveRamp is a data company listed on California's data broker registry. Here's how to ask it to remove you, for free. You don't need Free My ID to do this.

Step by step

  1. 1

    Open the privacy choices page.

  2. 2

    Under 'Deletion Request', click 'As a consumer'.

  3. 3

    Fill in the form on the page that opens and send it.

  4. 4

    For ads on your phone or browser, use the separate links at the top of the page.

  5. 5

    You can also call (844) 678-0045 and follow the spoken menu.

Open LiveRamp's removal page

Opens liveramp.com, LiveRamp's own website.

We checked these steps against LiveRamp's own page on 2026-11-07.

Or let Free My ID do it

Free My ID opens LiveRamp's removal page in your own browser, with these steps beside it and a Copy button for each box. On sites that allow it, it can fill the boxes in while you watch. You tick any "I'm not a robot" box yourself. Then it checks again later, because sites can list people again.
