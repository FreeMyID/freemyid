How to remove yourself from FreePeopleDirectory

FreePeopleDirectory is a people-search site. Sites like this build listings from public records and purchased data, often with your address, age, phone number and relatives. Here's how to ask it to remove you, for free. You don't need Free My ID to do this.

Good news: the Spokeo removal is meant to cover FreePeopleDirectory too. Start with the Spokeo guide, then check FreePeopleDirectory again in about a week.

You should never have to pay to be removed. If a people-search site asks for money or your Social Security number, stop.

Or let Free My ID do it

Free My ID sends the Spokeo request and later checks whether FreePeopleDirectory still lists you. Then it checks again later, because sites can list people again.
