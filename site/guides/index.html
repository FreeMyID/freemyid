Free opt-out guides

Step-by-step guides to ask people-search sites to remove your listing. They're free, and you don't need Free My ID to use them.

Before you start

  • Make a separate email address just for removals. Sites send confirmation links to it, and you won't mind if it gets spam.
  • You should never have to pay to be removed. If a people-search site asks for money or your Social Security number, stop.
  • Sites often list people again later. Check back every few months.
