Windows may show a blue warning. Here's why, and what to do

Windows shows "Windows protected your PC" for new programs that haven't paid for a publisher certificate yet. Free My ID is free and hasn't bought one yet. The warning doesn't mean anything is wrong.

  1. Open the file you downloaded, FreeMyIDSetup.exe.

  2. If a blue box says "Windows protected your PC", click the small words "More info".

  3. Check that it says the app is FreeMyIDSetup.exe, then click "Run anyway".

  4. Click Install. Free My ID opens by itself and walks you through six simple screens.

Stay safe: only download Free My ID from this page or from our GitHub releases page. We will never email you an installer, and we will never call you.

For helpers: check the download is genuine

Each release lists a SHA256SUMS.txt file. In PowerShell, run Get-FileHash .\FreeMyIDSetup.exe and check that the long code matches the line for FreeMyIDSetup.exe in that file.

Removing it

Open Windows Settings → Apps, find Free My ID, and click Uninstall. It asks whether to delete your saved records too.

Free My ID is free, open-source software that runs on your own computer. It is not a government service and is not affiliated with Facebook/Meta or any data broker.
