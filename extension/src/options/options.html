Free My ID settings

When you choose "Not now", the message stays hidden on that site for 30 days. This is remembered on this computer only.

What this add-on does and does not do

  • It sends nothing over the internet. No tracking, no statistics, no account.
  • It only talks to the Free My ID app on this computer. Your name and other details stay in the app; they are never given to this add-on.
  • On the sites in its list it looks at the site's address only. It does not read the page, and it does nothing on other sites.
  • It never fills in or clicks through "I am not a robot" checks. Those are always yours to do.
  • Some sites need steps only you can do, and a removed listing can come back. "Removed" always means removed from that one site.
